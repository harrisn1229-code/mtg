# m4 requests / known deviations

Every card in batch m4 is automated (`node game/test/lib-flagged.mjs m4` -> `flagged 0`). The items below are where the automation is an
approximation of the printed rules, or where an engine mechanic is only implemented inline for the cards that need it. Nothing was cleared
that is not implemented; these are the places a future engine pass could tighten.

## Approximations
- **Survivor's Med Kit** (m3): the RadAway player is chosen when the mode resolves, not as a target when the ability is put on the stack.
- **Vronos, Masked Inquisitor -2** and **Narset of the Ancient Way -2**: targets are chosen from `targetCandidates` (hexproof/shroud aware) while the
  ability resolves, not as targets on the stack. For Vronos this is only observable with 3+ players (one target per opponent). **Grist, the Hunger Tide -2**
  chooses the reflexive "destroy target" the same way.
- **Alchemist's Talent** level 3 ("if mana from a Treasure was spent"): mana in the pool is not tagged with its source. The engine remembers how much
  of the pool came from a Treasure (`pl.treasureMana`) and treats a spell as paid with Treasure mana when any of that mana was spent in the same
  phase (it can over-count if a player taps a Treasure early and pays with other floating mana).
- **Locke, Treasure Hunter**: "cast a spell from among those cards" allows exactly one spell (the first cast ends the permission for the rest).
- **Fortune Teller's Talent** level 1 ("look at the top card any time") is information-only: `g.canLookTop(p)` is true; the UI does not render a
  top-card peek yet (the top card is playable/castable from the actions list once level 2 applies).
- **Teyo** -2 direction (left/right) is asked only with 3+ players; in a 1v1 the choice is irrelevant.
- **Jace, Architect of Thought -2**: the opponent splits the cards by choosing pile 1 (pile 2 is the rest); the controller then chooses a pile.
- The AI never attacks a Battle and never uses Teyo's/Saheeli's abilities (they evaluate to 0); everything else is scored (see `AI_VALUE` in `m4.js`).

## Mechanics implemented only inline (owned by other batches)
- **Energy** (`Vault 112: Sadistic Simulation` and the other Vault Sagas that pay/gain {E}) and **voting** (`Vault 11: Voter's Dilemma`) are implemented as scripts inside the card
  overrides of this batch. If another batch adds a general energy/voting engine, those two inline scripts can be replaced.
